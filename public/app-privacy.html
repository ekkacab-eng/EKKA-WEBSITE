Ekka — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 August 2026 · Governed by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

Ekka ("Ekka", "we", "us") operates the Ekka ride-hailing mobile app and related services. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we protect it, and the rights you have. By using Ekka you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect and why

  • Phone number — to create your account and sign you in via one-time-password (OTP) verification.
  • Name and profile details — that you choose to add, to personalise your account.
  • Precise location — Riders: only during an active booking, to set your pickup, match a nearby driver, and track the trip. Drivers ("Captains"): while you are online, to receive nearby ride requests and share your live position with the rider on the trip. Collected only with your explicit in-app permission; you can turn it off in your device settings.
  • Trip and fare data — pickup/drop, route, fare and receipts, for the service, disputes, and safety review.
  • Payment details — we record how you paid and the transaction reference. We do not store card numbers or CVV.
  • Driver KYC documents (driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance, ID) — required by law to verify Captains. Encrypted at rest; every access by our staff is logged.
  • Emergency contacts — only if you add them, used for live trip sharing and SOS alerts.
  • Device and log data — basic technical logs needed to operate the service and prevent abuse.

2. How we use your data

  • Provide rides: matching, navigation, live tracking, fares and receipts.
  • Safety: SOS, trip sharing, and dispute resolution.
  • Account security: OTP login and fraud prevention.
  • Service improvement and required legal/tax record-keeping.

3. Third-party services we use

We share the minimum data needed with trusted providers who process it only on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — sends and verifies your login OTP.
  • Map and routing providers — to display maps, search places, and calculate routes and ETAs.
  • Razorpay — processes payments securely (PCI-DSS compliant). Ekka never sees your full card details.
  • Cloud hosting — stores app data on secured servers.

We do not sell your personal data, and we never share your phone number with the other party in a ride — calls and chat are masked through our platform.

4. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)

  • Access — request a copy of your data via in-app support.
  • Correction — edit your profile any time in the app.
  • Erasure — delete your account in-app (Profile → Settings → Delete my account). Your personal data is anonymised and your KYC documents are permanently purged.
  • Withdraw consent — disable location access in your device settings; features that require it will pause.
  • Grievance — contact our Data Grievance Officer (below); we respond within the timeframe required by law.

5. Data retention

Trip and payment records are retained only as long as required for tax, legal and dispute obligations. When you delete your account, personal identifiers are removed and KYC documents are purged.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). KYC documents are encrypted at rest and every access is logged. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised staff.

7. Children

Ekka is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a revised effective date. Continued use of Ekka after changes means you accept the updated policy.
